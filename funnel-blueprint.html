Phase mapping

Phase 1 - Days 1-3 (Core Funnel Install)

  • Lawn Care Operator Mentorship landing page deployed and live (HTML or Kajabi sub-page)
  • VSL placeholder uses verified Q+A episode (eHMQXaQZkLY) until Jason films the 3-4 minute mentorship trailer
  • Application form live (Typeform - 5-minute application + cohort interview routing)
  • Stripe + payment + email auto-trigger on application submit + cohort interview booking
  • Brand colours locked - landing applies the lawn-care green spine, hub stays in AG1 voice

By end of Day 3: Mentorship is bookable. Money flows on application acceptance.

Phase 2 - Days 4-7 (Insider Membership + IG Router)

  • Lawn Care Operator Insider product live on Kajabi (or Memberstack / Circle / Skool depending on Jason's preference)
  • Insider welcome sequence - 5 touches over 14 days
  • IG bio router live (Stan / Beacons / Linktree) - one-tap to $99 Letter, secondary tap to mentorship application
  • Pinned reel landing - bio router page as the IG-source landing for any reel with a CTA
  • Email list segmentation - source tag (IG / YouTube / Kajabi / Academy buyer) added on capture, used to route the welcome sequence

By end of Day 7: Insider is sellable. IG conversion path is live. Email list is segmented going forward.

Phase 3 - Post-Install · Ongoing (Content Compound)

  • Q+A archive packaging - the back-catalogue of @LawnCareLife Q+A shows organised inside the Insider membership by month + topic + warm-season operator stage
  • Reel cadence - 2-3 reels / wk leveraging the bio router (hook bank lives in content-strategy)
  • Quarterly warm-season briefing PDF - members-only commentary on chemistry / regulation / vendor changes
  • Mentorship cohort live calls - weekly cadence, route audit / pricing teardown / hiring conversation rotating

Phase 4 - Post-Install · Ongoing (Paid Traffic)

  • Pixel + GA4 installed on every page in the funnel
  • Retargeting tags - landing-page abandoners, application-starts-but-not-finished, $99 Letter cart abandoners
  • Top reel boost - top 5-10 organic reels boosted on Meta + YouTube pre-roll on warm-season lawn-care queries
  • Lead-flow target - scaling toward predictable cohort fill (12-25 / intake) + Insider growth

Integration points

  • YouTube end-screens - every Q+A and educational episode end-screen routes to lawncarelife.com mentorship landing for academy graduates, $99 Letter for new viewers
  • Pinned comment on YouTube - links to bio router for IG audience + Insider membership for academy graduates
  • Kajabi storefront tile - new "Mentorship" tile added to navigation alongside Mentoring Program / Weed Control / Mowing Business / Homeowners / Recommended Products
  • Email auto-tag on academy purchase - any new $497 buyer gets tagged "academy-grad" and added to a 30-day post-academy nurture pointing to mentorship + Insider
  • 3-free-gifts opt-in - flows into segmented welcome sequence by warm-season vs cool-season audience inferred from form question

Service-firm separation rules

  • Alabama Lawn Pros (alabamalawnpros.com) is the local service business. Untouched. No mentorship product references the local service.
  • Lawncarelife.com is the educator brand. All paid education products live here.
  • Mentorship copy never recruits Alabama-area homeowners (potential conflict with the service business).
  • The mentorship buyer is an LCO operator running their own warm-season service, not a homeowner.
