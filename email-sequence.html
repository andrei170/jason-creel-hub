Voice + tone notes (apply across all three sequences)

  • Plain-spoken Alabama operator. No internet-marketer tone.
  • Reference the Q+A by cadence ("every Tuesday"). Reference Alabama Lawn Pros as the live operating proof. Reference the Academy by name when it is the right moment.
  • Sign off with "Talk soon, Jason" - matches his Q+A on-camera close.
  • No payment-method mentions in these sequences. Payment surface lives on the Stripe checkout, not in the email copy.
  • No fabricated revenue claims or testimonials - the Year-1 floor numbers from bottleneck-analysis are conservative ranges, never round-numbered hype.
