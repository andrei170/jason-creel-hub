Script

Open (0:00 - 0:25)

"If you have already pulled the trigger on the $497 Weed Control + Fertilization Academy, you know what comes next. You have the warm-season chemistry handled. You have the application schedule. You have the route logic. And then the academy ends, and the work starts.

The lawn care operators who reach out to me most often are the ones who finished the academy six months ago, are running their second or third zoysia and bermuda season, and have hit the wall every operator hits at the same time."

Problem (0:25 - 1:00)

"It is not a knowledge wall. The chemistry works. The schedule works. The wall is the business behind the work.

It is the pricing decision you keep deferring because the spreadsheet has not caught up with the route. It is the first hire you should have already made. It is the truck you keep arguing with yourself about. It is the equipment ROI math you are doing in the field instead of in advance. And it is the question you cannot ask anyone you actually compete with, because everyone in your zip code who runs a warm-season lawn-care business is the wrong person to ask."

Story (1:00 - 1:45)

"I run Alabama Lawn Pros. Same fertilizer rotation, same warm-season grasses, same equipment list, same hire decisions. Every problem I teach you to solve in the academy I am solving in real time on my own routes.

The Q+A you watch every week on YouTube is the public version of how I think. The Lawn Care Letter is the print version of how I write the system down. The academy is how I hand you the chemistry. And what was missing - what operators have been asking for in the comments and the DMs for two years - is the room where you stop asking 'how do I run this' and start asking 'how do I scale this'."

Offer reveal (1:45 - 2:30)

"That room is the Lawn Care Operator Mentorship.

It is not a course. It is not a one-and-done. It is the cohort I would build if I had finished the academy and asked myself what I needed for the next 90 days, the next 12 months, and the season after that.

Live calls every week. Route audits, pricing teardown, hiring conversations, equipment ROI walked end-to-end with the receipts. The full warm-season SOP library. Vendor introductions to the suppliers I use. And the same room every other warm-season operator who is scaling past their first six figures is sitting in."

Pillars at a glance (2:30 - 3:00)

"Three pillars over the cohort.

Pillar one - the operator audit. We map your route, your application schedule, your pricing, your overheads, your hire plan against the warm-season operators who are 12-24 months ahead of you. By week four you have a finished business plan that survives the next two seasons.

Pillar two - the install. Pricing locked. First hire scoped. Equipment plan staged. Fertilizer + herbicide vendor map locked. By week eight the route is built to scale.

Pillar three - the playbook. The full warm-season SOP library, the seasonal calendar, the hiring scripts, and the route-to-six-figures math. By week twelve you have the playbook running, not the playbook waiting."

Close (3:00 - 3:45)

"The application is below. It takes five minutes. We open intakes twice a year and we cap each cohort small enough that the live calls are real.

If you are a warm-season operator with the academy under your belt and the next 12 months sitting in front of you, this is the next conversation. Apply below."

Production notes

  • Setting: Either Jason at his desk in studio (matches the YouTube Q+A look) or Jason on a route in Alabama (matches the alabamalawnpros.com brand). Field option is stronger for credibility - fertilizer truck, application gear, real lawn behind him.
  • Camera: One A-camera at chest height, eye-line just above the lens. Optional B-camera over-the-shoulder of equipment / route footage cutaways for pillar three (3-5 second inserts).
  • Audio: Lav mic on Jason. Backup shotgun on boom if filming outdoors.
  • Wardrobe: Either branded Alabama Lawn Pros polo (field) or Lawn Care Life t-shirt (studio). No suit, no jacket. Match the audience.
  • B-roll: 6-8 seconds each: spreader on a fall pre-emergent application, herbicide tank fill, route map open on iPad, invoice / pricing chart on a desk, Q+A live show counter ticking up.
  • End frame: Lawn Care Operator Mentorship wordmark + Apply CTA. Solid forest-green background. Five seconds.
  • Run time: 3:30-4:00 final cut. If the cut runs longer, trim the story section first, not the offer reveal.
  • First-cut delivery: within 7 days of kickoff. Until first cut is delivered, the landing page hero uses the verified Q+A placeholder (eHMQXaQZkLY).

Voice + tone notes

  • Plain-spoken Alabama operator. No US "guru" hype.
  • Technical when technical is required (pre-emergent timing, route density, fertilizer N-P-K decisions). Plain when plain is enough.
  • Reference the academy by name. Reference the Q+A by cadence. Reference Alabama Lawn Pros as the live operating proof.
  • Avoid: "secret," "hack," "shortcut," "blueprint," anything that sounds like an internet course pitch.
  • Use: "the room," "the cohort," "the playbook," "the install."
